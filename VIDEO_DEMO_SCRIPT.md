# Nemo Voice Typing: 45-second demo

Record a real Windows desktop at 1080p. Set up the speech model before
recording, use a quiet microphone, and keep the floating mic visible. Show
the actual app and cursor; use a text editor or chat draft with no private
information. Add captions after recording so the typed words stay readable.

| Time | Screen action | Voiceover or caption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5s | Show the Windows desktop, floating mic, and Nemo icon. | “Voice typing for Windows, without the cloud.” |
| 5–13s | Click the violet mic; show the active state. | “Click the mic or press Ctrl+Alt+A.” |
| 13–27s | Focus a blank text field and dictate: “Write your next idea by speaking. New line. It appears wherever your cursor is. Period” | “Dictate into the app you already use.” |
| 27–35s | Say “scratch that,” then dictate a short replacement sentence. | “Fix a sentence by voice.” |
| 35–41s | Click the mic to stop; show the tray icon and personal dictionary menu briefly. | “Your speech stays on your PC after the model download.” |
| 41–45s | End on the icon and project name. | “Nemo Voice Typing. Free and open source.” |

Use charcoal `#1A1B22`, violet `#6A5CFF`, white text, and a restrained red
`#E25C5C` only for the active recording state. Keep cuts simple and zoom in
only enough to make the floating control and dictated text legible. The mic
is genuinely clickable in the app; show a real cursor click or hotkey, not a
fake interaction overlay.
